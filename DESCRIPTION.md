# Material theme

Make a Material 3 color palette from one color, and add it to your site as design tokens.

## How to use it

1. Open the extension from its details page, or click its button in the toolbar.
2. Choose a source color.
3. Check the colors it makes, then add them to your site.

## Permissions

- **Open popups**: to show the palette.
- **Add and edit design tokens**: to save the colors as tokens. Tokens change how your published
  pages look.
